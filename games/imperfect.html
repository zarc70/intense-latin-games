Arena of the Dimachaerus

Welcome to the sands, Gladiator. You wield two swords. To survive, you must master the Imperfect Tense Latin verb endings.

  • Read the English phrase (e.g., "we were loving").
  • Complete the Latin stem (e.g., "ama") by typing the correct imperfect ending (e.g., "bamus").
  • Press ENTER or wait for the timer to expire to strike or block!
  • Odd attempts (STRIKE): Success makes the timer 0.1s faster. Failure costs a life.
  • Even attempts (BLOCK): Type correctly to defend. Failure costs a life.

You have 10 lives. The timer starts at 5.0 seconds and floors at 0.3s. Eventually, you will lose... but how many can you defeat?
